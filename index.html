Calculator Builder

Everything runs in this page. Your screenshots never leave your device.

Three steps:
1. Screenshots. I show you one thing to do at a time. You switch to your Calculator, do it, take a screenshot, and come back.
2. Upload. You pick all the screenshots at once.
3. Check. I show each one and you tap ✓ or ✗.
Use the iPhone Calculator in portrait, dark mode. Leave this page open in its tab while you work. It remembers your place.
Tip: open this same link on a computer to see every step there, so you do not have to keep switching back and forth. Then come back here to upload.
